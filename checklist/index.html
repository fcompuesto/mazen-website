Free checklist

The 5-minute small business cybersecurity checklist.

28 yes-or-no checks that show where your business is exposed. Tick what is true for your whole team today, add up your score, and see what to fix first.

What it covers

Six areas, about a minute each.

1. Accounts and passwords

Unique passwords, a password manager, multi-factor authentication, limited admin rights and removing former staff.

2. Email and phishing

Spam filtering, checking links and senders, confirming payment changes by phone, and a simple way to report suspicious email.

3. Devices and updates

Automatic updates, security software, encrypted laptops and phones, and knowing every device that touches business data.

4. Backups and data

Daily automatic backups, the 3-2-1 rule, a recent test restore, and knowing where personal information is stored.

5. Network and Wi-Fi

Router passwords and firmware, a separate guest network, strong Wi-Fi encryption and secure remote access.

6. People and response plan

A named security lead, need-to-know access, a one-page “if we are hacked” plan and offline emergency contacts.

How to use it

Score yourself in three steps.

  1. Tick. Check each box that is true for the whole business, not just for you.
  2. Count. Add up your ticked boxes out of 28.
  3. Act. Use the score table on page 3 to see what to fix first.
Good to know

A starting point, not an audit.

The checklist is general guidance to help you spot common gaps. It is not a full security assessment. If you want someone to check these areas with you and fix what is missing, a cybersecurity review is the next step.

Found some gaps?

Send your score and the boxes you could not tick. You will get a direct reply from the owner with what to fix first.
