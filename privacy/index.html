Privacy Policy

What personal information MaZen IT Solutions collects, why, who sees it, and how you can reach us about it.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

MaZen IT Solutions is a sole proprietorship operated by Farzen Compuesto in Metro Vancouver, British Columbia. This policy explains how the business handles personal information under British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). In this policy, "we" and "us" mean MaZen IT Solutions.

Who to contact

Farzen Compuesto is responsible for privacy at MaZen IT Solutions. Send questions, access requests, corrections, withdrawals of consent, and complaints to:

  • Email: fcompuesto@mazenitsolutions.ca
  • Phone: +1 (604) 445-3874

What we collect

Through the contact form on this website: your name, company, business email, phone number, the type of help you need, your approximate number of users, urgency, location, and the message you write.

By email, phone, or in person: the contact details and business information you share when you ask about or arrange services.

When we provide IT services: contact and billing details, and technical information about your systems that we need to do the work, such as network layouts, device lists, settings, and logs. We ask only for the access the work requires.

Website visitors: we do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this website. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your inquiry and decide whether and how we can help.
  • To quote, schedule, and deliver services, and to support you afterward.
  • To invoice, keep business and tax records, and meet legal obligations.
  • To protect this website and the business from misuse, spam, and security incidents.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We use it only for the purposes above, unless we ask for your consent for something else.

Your consent

When you submit the contact form or contact us directly, you are asking us to respond, and we treat that as your consent to use your details for the purposes in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual limits. If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to continue a service that depends on that information.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the website and the business, and only as needed:

  • Formspree processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Fontshare supplies the typefaces on this website, so it receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Our email provider handles the messages you send us.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or when you ask us to share it with a third party, such as another IT provider you work with.

Storage outside Canada

Some of these providers are based in, or may process data in, the United States or other countries outside Canada. Information held there may be subject to those countries' laws, including access by their authorities.

How long we keep it

InformationKept for

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for the sensitivity of the information, including account access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website and its contact form. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords or other highly sensitive credentials through the contact form or by ordinary email.

Your rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and how we have used and shared it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent, or ask us to delete information we no longer need.

We respond to access requests within 30 business days, and we will tell you if we need more time and why.

Questions and complaints

Please contact us first at the email or phone number above, and we will look into your concern. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
