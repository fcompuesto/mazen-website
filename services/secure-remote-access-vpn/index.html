Secure remote access and VPN

Secure remote access for people who do not work at the office.

Reach shared files and systems from home or another office without leaving remote desktop or file shares open to the internet.

Book a consultation Call (604) 445-3874
What is included

The work, in plain terms.

  • VPN design on your firewall, using WireGuard, OpenVPN, or IPsec to suit your equipment and users
  • Per-person accounts, with multi-factor authentication where supported
  • Access limited to what each person needs
  • Site-to-site links between offices
  • Client setup on laptops and phones, with simple instructions for your team
  • Testing, documentation, and a clear process for removing access when someone leaves
Who it is for

A good fit if you are…

  • Hybrid teams that need the office network or shared files from home
  • Owners who currently reach the office through an exposed remote desktop port, which is a common way attackers get in
  • Businesses with two or more locations that need to share resources
How it works

From first message to handoff.

  1. Scope. You list who needs access, from where, and to what.
  2. Design. MaZen chooses the VPN approach and access rules that fit your firewall and team.
  3. Set up and test. Accounts and devices are configured and tested from outside your office.
  4. Hand over. Your team gets short instructions, and you get documentation for adding and removing users.
Good to know

Often paired with a firewall review

Remote access is only as safe as the firewall it runs on. If your firewall is old or has accumulated rules nobody remembers, MaZen may recommend reviewing it as part of the same project.
