Business network setup

A business network that stays out of your way.

A well-planned office network is quiet when it works: Wi-Fi that holds up, guest and staff traffic kept apart, and a layout the next person can understand without guessing.

Book a consultation Call (604) 445-3874
What is included

The work, in plain terms.

  • Network design based on your space, devices, internet service, and plans for growth
  • Router or firewall, switch, and Wi-Fi access point selection and configuration
  • VLANs that separate staff devices, guests, printers, and servers
  • Wi-Fi with a separate guest network and sensible security settings
  • Guidance on cabling and equipment placement
  • Documentation: network diagram, IP address plan, device list, and admin access notes
Who it is for

A good fit if you are…

  • Offices moving into a new space or opening a second location
  • Teams outgrowing a consumer router, with dropouts and slow Wi-Fi
  • Businesses with a network that was never documented or planned
How it works

From first message to handoff.

  1. Discovery. You describe your users, devices, internet service, and what is frustrating today.
  2. Plan and quote. You receive a scoped plan with an equipment list, so you can buy what fits your budget.
  3. Install and configure. The work is done onsite or remotely and tested with your real devices.
  4. Handoff. You get the documentation and a walkthrough, with follow-up support available.
Good to know

Honest advice on equipment

MaZen recommends equipment that fits your size and budget, not the most expensive option. If your current gear is fine and only needs to be configured properly, you will be told that.
