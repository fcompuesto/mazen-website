Cybersecurity review

A cybersecurity review you can act on.

A practical look at how exposed your business is, ending in a prioritized list of fixes written in plain language, not a long report nobody reads.

Book a consultation Call (604) 445-3874
What is included

The work, in plain terms.

  • Devices: operating system updates, antivirus or endpoint protection, and disk encryption
  • Accounts: password practices, multi-factor authentication coverage, and who has admin rights
  • Email: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC settings that help stop spoofing of your domain
  • Backups: whether they exist, where they are, and whether a restore works
  • Network: firewall settings, what is visible from the internet, and Wi-Fi security
  • A written summary of findings ranked high, medium, and low, with a walkthrough call
Who it is for

A good fit if you are…

  • Businesses with no internal IT lead
  • Companies whose insurer or clients are asking about their security
  • Teams that have had an incident, or a near miss, and want to close the gaps
  • Growing offices that want sound habits before they get bigger
How it works

From first message to handoff.

  1. Scope. You share your size, systems, and what you most want checked.
  2. Review. MaZen examines the agreed areas, with your permission and access.
  3. Report. You receive prioritized findings and recommended fixes in plain language.
  4. Decide. You choose what to fix yourself and what to hand off. Remediation help is scoped separately.
Good to know

What a review is not

A review shows the risks found at the time of the review. It is not a guarantee that your business is secure, and it is not a formal audit or certification such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
