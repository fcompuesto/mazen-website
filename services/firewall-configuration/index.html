Firewall configuration

Firewall configuration with pfSense and OPNsense.

Your firewall is the front door of your network. MaZen sets up business-grade open-source firewalls so only the traffic you intend gets in or out.

Book a consultation Call (604) 445-3874
What is included

The work, in plain terms.

  • pfSense or OPNsense installed and configured on suitable hardware or a virtual machine
  • Firewall rules built around your real needs, with inbound traffic blocked by default
  • Network segmentation with VLANs for staff, guests, and devices that need to be kept apart
  • DHCP, DNS, and basic traffic controls
  • Remote access and site-to-site VPN, where you need them
  • Review and cleanup of an existing firewall's rules
  • Configuration backup and a plain-language handoff document
Who it is for

A good fit if you are…

  • Offices protected only by an ISP modem or a consumer router
  • Businesses that inherited a firewall nobody dares to touch
  • Teams that want business-grade control without per-user licence fees, since OPNsense and pfSense Community Edition have none
How it works

From first message to handoff.

  1. Review. You share your network layout, internet service, and what must stay reachable.
  2. Design. MaZen proposes rules and segmentation in plain language before anything changes.
  3. Cutover. The new firewall is configured, tested, and switched in at a time that suits your office.
  4. Handoff. You receive the configuration backup, documentation, and update guidance.
Good to know

Why pfSense and OPNsense

Both are mature, widely used open-source firewall platforms that run on commodity hardware. They offer the segmentation, VPN, and logging features small businesses need, and they can be maintained by any qualified administrator, so you are not tied to a single vendor.
