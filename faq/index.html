FAQ

Common questions, answered plainly.

What to expect when you work with MaZen IT Solutions. If your question is not here, send a message and the owner will reply directly.

Who does MaZen IT Solutions work with?

MaZen works with small businesses, professional offices, home offices, and distributed teams in Metro Vancouver that need dependable IT without the overhead of a large provider.

Where do you work?

MaZen serves Vancouver, Burnaby, New Westminster, Surrey, Delta, Coquitlam, Port Moody, Port Coquitlam, Langley, and Richmond. Work is done onsite where it helps and remotely where it does not need a visit.

When are you available?

Evenings and weekends are part of the schedule, so work can be planned around your business hours. Typical availability is weekday evenings from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. and weekends from 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. If something urgent happens, call and urgent call-outs can be arranged.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the size and scope of the work, so it is not listed on the website. Send a message describing your business and what you need, and you will get a direct reply from the owner.

What happens when I book a consultation?

You describe your business, the number of users, and the problem or goal. MaZen asks follow-up questions, explains the options in plain language, and recommends a next step. You decide whether to go ahead.

Do you offer ongoing monthly support?

Yes. Monthly support covers recurring issues, device onboarding, office changes, and technology planning. Many clients start with a single project, such as a network or firewall setup, and move to ongoing support afterward.

What should I do if I think we have been hacked?

Disconnect the affected devices from the network, do not pay or reply to any demand, write down what you saw, and call (604) 445-3874. The malware cleanup and incident response page has a short checklist.

Will you need access to our systems and passwords?

MaZen asks only for the access the work requires. Sharing credentials through the contact form or ordinary email is not recommended. Temporary accounts, or credentials shared through a secure method that you and MaZen agree on, are better, and access can be removed when the work ends.

Can you work alongside our current IT provider or staff?

Yes. MaZen can handle a specific project, such as a firewall or network upgrade, while your existing provider or staff keep running day-to-day IT.

Can you guarantee we will never be breached?

No one can. A security review or hardening work reduces risk and shows you where the gaps are, but it is not a guarantee. The goal is practical protection and a clear plan if something does go wrong.

Do I need a server at all?

Not always. Many small offices do well with cloud file storage and no server. If that fits you better, MaZen will say so before you spend money on hardware.

How is my information handled?

Information you share is used only to respond to you and deliver services. The Privacy Policy explains what is collected, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to ask questions or make a request.

Still have a question?

Share your business, the number of users, and what you need. You will get a direct reply from the owner.
